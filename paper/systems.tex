\section*{Appendix\quad Measured inference and reproducibility}
\topic{Latency.} We benchmarked the retained 118K five-minute model and the 30M extended seed-11 model after the study's training processes had cleared. For each checkpoint we compared its actual learned policy to forced depth on the same weights. During six warmups and 48 timed rounds, modes were interleaved in a deterministic shuffled order per batch size. Each call predicted 48 positions in 64-byte test contexts and synchronized the logits on the M4. These are whole-window scores, distinct from next-byte decoding rates. Peak bytes refer to the MLX allocator and include the active model, preloaded inputs and temporary tensors. The operating system and other applications remained live.

\topic{Interpreting the measurement.} A host decision at each possible halt can add synchronization and dispatch overhead. Even when the adaptive and forced policies execute the same number of recurrent blocks, their elapsed times can differ. The profile records retain each timed call. The effects of hardware contention on the discovery loop are visible in the separate update-matched validation replay.

\topic{What can be reproduced locally.} The receipt archive includes every estimator and seed, compressed Boolean-program predictions, model and data checksums, per-fit counts, all search trials and the original protocol digests. Two seed-11 language checkpoints reproduced their full test BPB and exit histograms after reloading. Mutating bytes that follow a prediction left its earlier logits unchanged. A dedicated mixed-depth check exercised batch removal and reordering across all four exits. An independent replay regenerated the main component and factorial endpoints. The 100M-scale intermediate weights were discarded after evaluation. Original predictions and hazard records remain available, as do the replay discrepancies described in Section 6.

\topic{Working files.} The project root contains \texttt{README.md} with rerun commands and \texttt{MODEL\_CARD.md} with the supported input formats. \texttt{results/verification.json} inventories saved weights. \texttt{results/lm/inference\_profile.json} contains the timed call samples. The editable manuscript and figures are under \texttt{paper/}. The complete replication archive includes the large local weights.
