# Integrated controller study, experimental protocol

Frozen before training on 2026-11-21. Experiment 5 is a controlled, sequential associative-program classification benchmark. Experiment 6 uses counterfactual policies on the same models. These are research-scale mechanism tests. The backbone is a small recurrent MLP over a compressed query, distinct from the main recurrent Transformer.

## Data and information boundary

Each example has a four-dimensional header, eight independently sampled symbols (alphabet of four), then a binary query. Four independent latent bits choose the reasoning length (one or three), adjacent versus interleaved pairing, one of two class permutations and which half of the past stream is relevant. The header encodes these bits in a fixed orthonormal Hadamard basis with Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.15. The query chooses one of the two pairs in the relevant half. The answer starts at the sum of the two symbols modulo four; apply the selected permutation one or three times. The permutation maps are [1,2,3,0] and [0,2,3,1]. No hidden bits, execution traces or action labels supervise the network. The procedural structure deliberately favors adaptive storage and grouping and admits a hand-engineered solution. Header signals are supplied to every backbone. Storage and grouping controllers see the header before any query. The future query has no path into either choice.

## Model and design

D chooses one or three repetitions of a shared nonlinear residual block. The fixed depth is two. G chooses adjacent or interleaved pairing inside the four retained symbols. Fixed grouping is adjacent. The compressed representation is the sum of the two one-hot symbol vectors in each pair. R chooses one of two independently trainable recurrent expert matrices; fixed routing selects the query-index expert, independent of latent task. M selects the earlier or later four-token bank; fixed retention keeps the later bank. At inference, materialize one retained bank, compress to two states, read the queried state, encode that result with the header into 24 hidden units, execute one expert per step and predict one of four classes. The shared controller is a 4-to-16 tanh layer and four binary outputs from the header. Hard routing uses logit sign except for depth. Learned depth ranks scores within a fixed cohort of 64; exactly half receive one step and half three. The batch quota gives every cell precisely two steps per example on average. It requires cohort inference. The depth score is computed before observing symbols or the query.

All 16 binary combinations of learned D, G, R and M are trained from scratch, plus a domain-informed control that decodes the header with the known inverse and configures the four functions. Its backbone is trained with the same optimizer; its depth obeys the same cohort quota. Fixed and adaptive cells allocate the same backbone and two experts. Every run uses the same 16 configuration-independent forward branches (four readouts times two experts with two selectable adaptive depths, with intermediate two-step logits also available); exact expected categorical cross-entropy under independent Bernoulli decisions, Adam learning rate 0.012, 240 minibatches of 64, float32, no checkpoint selection. Add 0.25 times squared deviation of mean soft depth probability from 0.5 when D is active. Runs use three independent paired training seeds 41, 42, 43. Every cell sees the same examples and initial weights within seed. The test set is an independent, fixed sequence of 4,096 examples shared by all models. Model weights are frozen before evaluating. Retain final accuracy, cross-entropy, hard-action frequencies and physical hard-dispatch equivalence. Report training time, examples, trainable versus allocated parameters, estimated dense-training linear MACs, deployed active inference MACs and fixed-overhead standardized MACs. The training MAC estimate uses triple forward linear MACs for forward plus reverse; nonlinearities, data movement, optimizer, gather, sorting and normalization are excluded. More precise operations or timings may differ. All factorial cells execute the standardized four-output controller at inference for exact nominal MAC comparisons. Additionally report deployable costs with unused controller heads or the entire controller removed. Full and intervention paths share both raw and standardized MACs.

## Endpoints and inference

The primary synergy contrast is full accuracy minus the sum of four singleton accuracies plus three times baseline accuracy. Test hierarchical interpretation using all six two-factor difference-in-differences averaged over settings of other axes. Report mean contrasts and 95 percent Student t intervals across the three paired training seeds (2 degrees of freedom). Intervals describe variability of training with one shared test set; secondary contrasts are exploratory and unadjusted. Full exceeding the best individual variant alone does not establish synergy. Summarize the quality versus optimized-cost frontier at the measured budget. Compare model actions to hidden factors only after training, accounting for arbitrary expert-label swaps, and compare the full policy to a whole-action-tuple permutation within each cohort plus one-head permutations for D, G, R and M. Permutations preserve per-head frequencies and the full joint histogram for the tuple intervention. Compare forced one- versus three-step accuracy for each independent composition length. Inference latency is descriptive; other study suites may share the machine. The study stops at the predeclared 51 fitted models. No test-guided retries or hyperparameter changes. Report weak, null and negative results.
