# Scaling extension registered before any scaling evaluation

The initial profiling and paper request motivate a longer, separately evaluated replication. The initial width-2880 three-update training-only profile took 0.093 seconds per warmed batch of two. It had no development or final evaluation. Based on this timing, predefine 160 updates, batch eight, length 32, at 896, 1600 and 2880 width (approximately 10M/30M/100M parameters), with seeds 17, 29, and 43 and all five independently trained models (shared fixed-depth 1, 2, 3, 4; adaptive). Every fit receives 1,280 examples/40,960 tokens and is initialized from scratch. These are still limited data and update horizons. Apply a common width-scaled peak Adam learning rate of 0.0003 * 896 / width, with a linear 16-update warmup from 10% followed by cosine decay to 10% at the last update. Use gradient clipping with global norm 1.0. BF16 parameters and arithmetic, FP32 losses. Keep the adaptive expected-depth penalty at 0.012.

Use disjoint fresh training stream offsets and distinct locked development (128 examples)/test (256 examples) streams for this extension. One development threshold per adaptive checkpoint targets 2.5 expected hard passes. Compare expected FLOPs and accuracy to all fixed points and randomized fixed mixtures; use the highest accuracy at or below the adaptive cost, plus the strict same-FLOP linear mixture where supported. Report the untuned 0.5 hazard threshold as secondary. Use paired seed mean and Student-t intervals, and independently real early stopping. Separately compare train FLOPs, actual parameter counts and the raw fixed-four accuracy point. Fixed-four and adaptive training cost are almost identical analytically. Publish all 45 fits independent of direction, with no additional tuning on test outcomes.
